I am an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at Virginia Tech, based in the greater Washington, D.C., metropolitan area. I am also a [Commonwealth Cyber Initiative (CCI)](https://cyberinitiative.org/) Faculty Fellow and an affiliate of the [Lawrence Berkeley National Lab](https://www.lbl.gov/).
Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Simons Institute for the Theory of Computing at UC Berkeley, hosted by [Umesh Vazirani](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~vazirani/) and [Lin Lin](https://lin.caltech.edu/). I earned my B.Sc. from the University of Hong Kong and my Ph.D. from the University of Maryland in 2024 under the supervision of [Xiaodi Wu](https://www.cs.umd.edu/~xwu/). My name in Chinese is 冷佳奇.

My research lies at the intersection of **quantum computation**, **machine learning**, and **optimization**. I design quantum algorithms to overcome efficiency barriers in science, engineering, and AI, with an emphasis on **algorithm–software–hardware co-design** for near-term and early fault-tolerant quantum computers. I am also increasingly interested in quantum-inspired methods that can leverage modern classical accelerators, including GPUs.
